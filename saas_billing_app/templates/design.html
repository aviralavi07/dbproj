Clients and plans are linked many to many through SUBSCRIPTION, which holds a foreign key to each.

Rules enforced by the database

  • Every table has a primary key.
  • Nine foreign keys stop a record from pointing to a parent that does not exist. A parent row cannot be deleted while child rows exist, except that the lines of an invoice are deleted with the invoice.
  • Client e-mail, plan name and the subscription in a cancellation must be unique.
  • Amounts, quantities and limits must be positive, status columns accept fixed values only, and dates must be in a sensible order, for example End_Date cannot be before Start_Date.

Normalization

The tables are in third normal form. Plan details are stored only in PRICING_TIER, client details only in CLIENT, and the lines of an invoice are stored in INVOICE_ITEM instead of being repeated inside INVOICE. Total_Amount and Amount are stored on purpose, so that an old invoice never changes when a plan price changes. Two audit queries check that they always match the lines.

Views
